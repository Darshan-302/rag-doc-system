Quasar Fitness Data Privacy Notice. Quasar Fitness collects heart rate, step counts and sleep duration from your wristband to provide training insights. Personal health data is encrypted at rest and is never sold to advertisers. Users can download all of their data or request permanent erasure from the Privacy tab, and erasure is completed within 30 days. Anonymised statistics may be shared with university research partners. Data is retained for 24 months after an account is closed.
