## 1. Purpose of this Form

This example form illustrates the general sections a patient consent
document might contain when a healthcare provider uses a RAG-based
informational assistant as part of care delivery. It is a structural
template only.

## 2. Example Consent Sections

- [ ] I consent to my de-identified health information being used to support
      a documentation/retrieval assistant used by my care team.
- [ ] I understand this assistant provides informational support only and
      does not replace my clinician's judgment.
- [ ] I understand I may withdraw this consent at any time by notifying
      [Organization Contact / Privacy Officer], and that withdrawal will stop
      future use of my information in this system (see
      docs/HEALTHCARE_RAG_GUIDE.md, "Consent Management Notes").
- [ ] I have been given the opportunity to ask questions about how my
      information will be used and protected.
