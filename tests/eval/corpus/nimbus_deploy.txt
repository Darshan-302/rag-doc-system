Nimbus Platform Deployment Runbook. Production deployments of the Nimbus platform happen through the canary pipeline: first 5 percent of traffic goes to the new build for 15 minutes, then 50 percent, then everything. If the error rate rises above 2 percent the pipeline automatically rolls back to the previous release. Deployments are frozen on Fridays after noon and during the last week of December. Database migrations must be backward compatible with the prior release. The on-call engineer approves every production release.
