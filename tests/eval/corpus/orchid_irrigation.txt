Orchid House Watering Guide. Phalaenopsis orchids in the Orchid House should be watered once a week by soaking the roots for ten minutes and letting them drain fully. Never let water collect in the crown of the plant because it causes rot. Use rainwater or filtered water at room temperature. Feed with diluted fertilizer every second watering from spring to autumn. In winter reduce watering to once every ten days. Silver green roots mean the orchid is thirsty while plump green roots mean it is hydrated.
