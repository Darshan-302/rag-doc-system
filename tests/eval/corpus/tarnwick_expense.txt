Tarnwick Industries Travel Expense Policy. Employees of Tarnwick Industries must book flights at least 10 days before departure and fly economy class for trips under six hours. Hotel stays are reimbursed up to 140 credits per night. Meals are covered by a daily allowance of 45 credits and alcohol is never reimbursable. Receipts must be uploaded to the Tarnwick expense tool within 21 days of the trip. Claims above 2000 credits require approval from a director.
