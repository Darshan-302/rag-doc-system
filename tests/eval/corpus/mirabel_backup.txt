Mirabel Storage Backup and Recovery Guide. Mirabel Storage takes incremental snapshots of every volume each night at 02:30 and a full snapshot every Sunday. Snapshots are kept for 35 days and replicated to a second datacenter in the Tolvane zone. To restore, an administrator selects a snapshot in the console and chooses restore to new volume; a typical 500 GB volume is restored in about 25 minutes. The recovery point objective is 24 hours and the recovery time objective is 2 hours.
