Lumen X2 Headlamp Battery Care. The Lumen X2 headlamp uses a 2200 mAh lithium ion cell that gives about 12 hours on the low beam and 3 hours on turbo. Charge it with the supplied USB-C cable; a full charge takes roughly 4 hours and the indicator light turns from red to green. Avoid storing the headlamp in temperatures above 45 degrees Celsius. If the lamp will be unused for months, keep the battery at about half charge. The cell is rated for 500 charge cycles before capacity drops to 80 percent.
