Vorpal Gadgets Returns Policy. Customers of Vorpal Gadgets may send back any unopened gizmo within 45 days of delivery for a full refund. Opened gizmos can be exchanged within 14 days if the sealed warranty card is intact. To start a return, open a ticket in the Vorpal customer portal and print the prepaid blue label. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 6 business days after the warehouse in Quillhaven scans the parcel. Custom engraved gizmos cannot be returned. Shipping fees for the original order are not refunded unless the item arrived damaged.
