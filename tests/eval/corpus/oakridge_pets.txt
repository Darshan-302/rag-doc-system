Oakridge Apartments Pet Rules. Residents of Oakridge Apartments may keep up to two cats or one dog under 25 kilograms per unit. A refundable pet deposit of 300 credits is due before the animal moves in. Dogs must be leashed in all common areas and owners must clean up after them immediately. Exotic animals, including snakes and parrots, are not allowed. Repeated noise complaints about a pet can lead to a written warning and, after three warnings, removal of the pet permit.
