Kelpie Cloud Password and Login Security. Kelpie Cloud requires passwords of at least 14 characters. Two factor authentication through a hardware key or an authenticator app is mandatory for administrators. After five failed sign in attempts the account is locked for 20 minutes. Sessions expire after 8 hours of inactivity. Passwords are stored with the argon2id hashing algorithm and are never emailed in plain text. Security incidents should be reported to the Kelpie response desk within one hour of discovery.
