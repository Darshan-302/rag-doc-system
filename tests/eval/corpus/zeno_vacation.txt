Zenobia Labs Time Off Handbook. Every full-time employee at Zenobia Labs accrues 1.75 vacation days per month, which is 21 days per year. Unused vacation days roll over until a cap of 30 days is reached. Staff must submit leave requests in the Zenobia people system at least two weeks ahead, and their team lead approves them. Sabbatical leave of four paid weeks is granted after seven years of service. Public holidays follow the calendar of the Marnovia region and are not deducted from the vacation balance.
