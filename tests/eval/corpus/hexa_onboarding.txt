Hexagon Bakery New Baker Onboarding. On the first day a new baker at Hexagon Bakery receives a uniform, a locker key and a tour of the proofing room. The first week is spent shadowing a senior baker on the early shift that starts at 04:00. Food safety certification must be completed within 30 days. Each new baker is paired with a mentor for three months. Sourdough starter duty is assigned only after the probation period ends. Flour deliveries arrive on Tuesdays and Fridays.
